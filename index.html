For My Abhi

Every little memory has a place in my heart.

Are you ready to open our little universe?

Chapter 1: The Beginning

It all began on October 30, 2021, during the COVID days. I was in class 9, and neither of us knew how important that first conversation would become.

We started talking and decided to become best friends. You were shy about talking to girls, and I still remember our first call.

When school reopened, you saw me, but I didn't recognise you. I even waited to hear your name during attendance, but there were two Abhis in class!

We were shy in person, so we continued talking through messages when we got home.

Chapter 2: Becoming Us

Slowly, you became someone very special to me. I used to feel jealous when you talked to other girls, and sometimes we fought over little things.

You confessed your feelings first. At first, I said no because I was afraid of losing our friendship. But you didn't give up on me.

Finally, on February 23, 2022, we became US. ❤️

Even when we were only friends, I would miss you so much when you didn't come to class.

I still remember our first kiss on a special class day. It was unexpected and a memory I'll always treasure.

Chapter 3: Little Memories

I'll never forget our class 10 farewell, when I secretly put a sweet in your mouth without anyone noticing. It was our little moment. 💗

We hoped we could study together in +1. We visited a school and submitted our forms. That day, you held my hand, something I had secretly wished for.

We couldn't attend the same school, but we still found ways to see each other in the evenings.

I remember you running towards me during a break and asking for my hand. When you held it and looked at me, my heart felt so full.

And when you suddenly rested your head on my shoulder, my heart beat so fast. Those little moments mean so much.

Chapter 4: Through Everything

Our journey hasn't always been easy. Sometimes we fought over little things, and sometimes I was afraid of the problems our relationship might bring.

Even when things became difficult, you comforted me. We faced misunderstandings, family worries, and times when we couldn't talk to each other.

We even hoped to study together in college, but things didn't work out the way we wanted. After some silence, we found our way back to each other.

That silence helped me understand how much I love you and how much our relationship means to me.

My happiest memories are the ones where you were there. Even on difficult days, being with you made me happy. You're one of the most precious gifts life has given me.
